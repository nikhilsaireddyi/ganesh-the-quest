## 🖥️ Detailed Guide: How the PC Keyboard Works

On PC / Desktop, **no on-screen touch buttons are rendered**, giving you a clean, cinematic display:

1. **Walking & Exploration**:
   - Press **`A`** or **`Left Arrow`** to walk left toward the street entrance, residences, and the Mango Tree.
   - Press **`D`** or **`Right Arrow`** to walk right toward the main Mandapam pandal, generator, and decoration spots.
2. **Sprinting (`Shift`)**:
   - Hold down **`Left Shift`** or **`Right Shift`** while walking. Your character will dash forward with festive speed.
3. **Interacting (`E` or `Space`)**:
   - Stand near an NPC (Festival Uncle, Child Ananya, Electrician, Flower Seller) or interactive area (Pandal Foundation, Generator, Mango Tree) and press **`E`** or **`Space`** to talk or begin mini-games.
   - During dialogues, press **`E`** or click anywhere inside the dialogue box to read through speeches.
4. **Throwing Holy Gulal (`Space`)**:
   - While walking down the street or dancing in the procession, press **`Space`** to splash bright gulal powders into the air. Devotees around you will celebrate!
5. **Photo Mode (`C`)**:
   - Press **`C`** anytime during gameplay to pull up the festival camera with viewfinder grid and focus reticle.
   - Press **`C`** again (or click the orange shutter button) to capture a clean, full-resolution 16:9 photograph.
   - Photos are stored in your **Scrapbook** and can be downloaded directly to your PC.
   - Press **`Escape`** or click `EXIT ✕` to close the camera.
6. **Wardrobe (`W`)**:
   - Press **`W`** to preview and change your character's festival attire (Traditional Saffron Kurta, Royal Blue Kurta, Golden Festive Silk, or Eco-Cotton White).
7. **Badges (`B`)**:
   - Press **`B`** to inspect unlocked festival achievements, milestones, and challenges.
8. **Mouse Puzzles**:
   - All minigames (connecting colored wires, cooking modaks, plucking mango leaves, assembling bamboo) use simple, intuitive mouse clicks and drags.

## 📱 Detailed Guide: How the Mobile Touch Buttons Work

On mobile smartphones and tablets (touchscreen devices in landscape orientation), intuitive tactical on-screen controls are displayed:

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [📸 PHOTO] [👕 WARDROBE] [🏆 BADGES]              [⛶ FULL] [🔊] [||]   |
|                                                                         |
|                          (GAME WORLD / CANVAS)                          |
|                                                                         |
|                                                          [🎨 GULAL]     |
|   [ ◀ ]   [ ▶ ]                                       [⚡ RUN]  [ ACT ] |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1. Movement Cluster (Bottom-Left)
- **`◀` Left Arrow**: Touch and hold with your left thumb to move left.
- **`▶` Right Arrow**: Touch and hold with your left thumb to move right.
- Both buttons support smooth multi-touch, so you can steer while using your right thumb for actions.

### 2. Action Cluster (Bottom-Right)
- **`ACT` Button**: Large golden button. Tap to interact with characters, pick up materials, inspect stations, and confirm dialogue prompts.
- **`⚡ RUN` Button**: Amber sprint button. Hold this down with your right thumb while moving to sprint at high velocity.
- **`🎨 GULAL` Button**: Positioned just above the action buttons. Tap to throw colorful holy gulal powder bursts across the screen.

### 3. Screen Touch in Minigames
During mini-games and dialogue scenes, on-screen movement buttons fade away automatically so you can touch the puzzles directly:
- **Mango Leaves Plucking**: Tap mango leaves on the sacred tree branches to pluck them directly into your leaf bag.
- **Electrical Wiring**: Touch a wire connector on the left and drag your finger across to the matching color terminal on the right.
- **Generator Repair**: Tap the circular hand crank repeatedly to fill the energy gauge.
- **Flower Rangoli**: Tap decorative petals and lamps to place them in the mandala pattern.
- **Modak Cooking**: Tap fragrant ingredients (grated coconut, jaggery, cardamom, ghee) into the holy cooking pot, then tap to shape the modaks.
- **Maha Aarti**: Tap the bell chime button or wave the sacred flame around Bappa.

### 4. Top Quick-Action HUD Chips
- Tap **`📸 PHOTO`** at the top-left to enter Photo Mode. Tap the circular shutter button at the bottom center to capture photos. Tap **`📖 SCRAPBOOK`** at the bottom-right to browse and download your pictures.
- Tap **`👕 WARDROBE`** to swap festival kurtas with a single tap.
- Tap **`🏆 BADGES`** to review quest trophies.
- Tap **`[✕]`** at the top-right of any modal (or tap outside the card) to dismiss it.

## 🗺️ Step-by-Step Festival Quest Walkthrough

Follow the animated golden guide arrow hovering over your active objectives:

### 1. Build the Mandapam Pandal
- **Location**: Right side of the street (Pandal foundation).
- **Objective**: Talk to **Festival Uncle** and assemble the bamboo support frame.
- **How to play**: Tap or click the bamboo poles in numbered sequence to erect the sturdy festival pandal foundation.

### 2. Pluck Fresh Mango Leaves (Toran)
- **Location**: The lush Mango Tree near the middle-left houses.
- **Objective**: Harvest fresh mango leaves to weave the auspicious door Toran garland.
- **How to play**: Tap or click 5 vibrant green mango leaves from the tree branches to collect them into your festival wicker bag.

### 3. Arrange the Rangoli & Flowers
- **Location**: In front of the Mandapam entrance.
- **Objective**: Talk to the **Flower Seller** to craft a divine floral Rangoli.
- **How to play**: Place flower petals and traditional motifs symmetrically into the central mandala.

### 4. Restore Street Lighting & Repair Generator
- **Location**: Transformer junction box & power generator on the far right.
- **Objective**: Connect the colored circuits with the **Electrician** and crank the generator.
- **How to play**:
  - **Wiring Puzzle**: Drag Red to Red, Blue to Blue, Yellow to Yellow, and Green to Green.
  - **Generator Crank**: Rapidly tap or click the rotary crank to build voltage and ignite the pandal's string lanterns!

### 5. Cook Holy Modaks with Child Ananya
- **Location**: Sweet-making stall near the houses.
- **Objective**: Prepare Lord Ganesha's favorite sweet offering (Ukadiche Modak).
- **How to play**: Add holy ingredients into the vessel, stir gently, and steam delicious golden modaks.

### 6. The Divine Reveal & Maha Aarti Ritual
- **Location**: The decorated Mandapam altar.
- **Objective**: Welcome the radiant clay idol of Lord Ganesha and perform the sacred evening Aarti.
- **How to play**: Wave the glowing brass camphor plate in gentle clockwise circles and ring the temple bells.

### 7. Synchronized Palanquin Lift
- **Location**: Mandapam dais.
- **Objective**: Gather the community to hoist the ceremonial palanquin on the count of three.
- **How to play**: Time your press with the rhythm meter to lift Lord Ganesha together!

### 8. Grand Festival Procession (Miravanuk)
- **Location**: Grand festive avenue toward the holy river.
- **Objective**: March toward the sacred ghat accompanied by music, cheering crowds, and dance troupes.
- **Interactive Mini-Events**:
  - **🥁 Dhol-Tasha Drumming**: Match drum beat cues in rhythm to drive the festive energy.
  - **🔔 Lezim Folk Dance**: Tap along with traditional cymbals to perform Maharashtra's folk dance.
  - **🎨 Gulal Splashes**: Press `Space` or tap `🎨 GULAL` to celebrate with colorful clouds of powder.

### 9. Sacred Visarjan Immersion
- **Location**: Sacred river ghat at twilight.
- **Objective**: Accompany Lord Ganesha aboard the ceremonial flower boat (*Naav*) into calm, moonlit deep waters for the holy farewell.
- Devotees chant *"Ganpati Bappa Morya, Pudhchya Varshi Lavkar Ya!"* as blessings descend under the full moon.
